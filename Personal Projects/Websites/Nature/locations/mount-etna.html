Overview

Mount Etna is Europe's most active volcano and one of the most studied on the planet. Its frequent effusive and explosive eruptions build and reshape its summit craters and lava fields.

Etna's fertile volcanic soils support extensive agriculture including vineyards and orchards on its lower slopes. The volcano is a UNESCO World Heritage Site recognized for scientific and cultural significance.

Key Facts

🔭 Laboratory Volcano

Continuous monitoring advances understanding of eruption mechanics.

🌋 Multiple Craters

Complex summit hosts several active vents emitting gas and lava.

🍇 Rich Soil

Volcanic deposits nurture renowned Sicilian wine terroirs.

🚀 Ash Plumes

Frequent explosive phases disrupt regional air traffic.

🔥 Lava Fountains

Spectacular strombolian and paroxysmal events attract visitors.

🛰️ Satellite Study

Space-based InSAR tracks inflation and deflation cycles.
