Overview

Eyjafjallajökull is a glacier‑capped stratovolcano in southern Iceland whose 2010 eruption disrupted trans‑Atlantic aviation with extensive ash clouds. The event highlighted the global impact of moderate eruptions when fine ash enters high‑traffic air corridors.

Beneath its ice lies a complex volcanic system influenced by Iceland's tectonic setting atop a spreading ridge and hotspot. Meltwater flooding, phreatomagmatic plumes, and ash dispersion characterized its notable eruptive sequence.
