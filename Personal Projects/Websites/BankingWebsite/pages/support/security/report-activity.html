Report Suspicious Activity

Protect your account by reporting any unauthorized or suspicious transactions

What to Report

Important

If you suspect immediate fraud or unauthorized access, contact our 24/7 emergency hotline: 1-800-LUNA-911

You should report any of the following:

  • Unrecognized transactions or charges
  • Suspicious login attempts or security alerts
  • Unexpected changes to your account information
  • Phishing emails or messages claiming to be from LUNA

Reporting Process

  1. Document the suspicious activity (date, time, amount)
  2. Take screenshots or photos of any relevant information
  3. Fill out the secure report form below
  4. Our security team will contact you within 1 hour

Preventive Measures

Security Tips

Enable two-factor authentication and biometric verification for enhanced account security

  • Regularly monitor your account activity
  • Update your password every 90 days
  • Never share your login credentials
  • Use the LUNA secure app for transactions
