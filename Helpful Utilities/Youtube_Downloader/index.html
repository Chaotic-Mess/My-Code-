🎥 YouTube Video Downloader

Download YouTube videos and audio in various formats and quality options — fast, free, and private.

What It Does

This tool uses the cobalt.tools API to fetch downloadable links for YouTube videos. It supports multiple formats (MP4, WebM, MP3), quality options, and even audio-only downloads. Everything runs in your browser — no data collection, no tracking, no nonsense.

  • Download videos in HD, Full HD, or 4K quality
  • Extract audio-only (MP3, M4A, Opus formats)
  • Choose video format (MP4, WebM)
  • Entirely client-side — your URLs never touch our servers
  • Free and no rate limits for normal use

How It Works

This tool uses a custom backend hosted on PythonAnywhere that runs yt-dlp, a powerful open-source YouTube downloader. Your browser sends the video URL to the backend, which processes it and returns a direct download link.

Privacy: Your video URL is processed by a personal backend server. No data is logged, stored, or shared with third parties. Everything is deleted after processing.

Notes & Tips

  • Works with standard YouTube videos, Shorts, and even some age-restricted content.
  • Audio-only mode extracts just the audio track (great for music or podcasts).
  • If a specific quality isn't available, cobalt will return the closest match.
  • Download speeds depend on your connection and the video size.
  • Some videos may be geo-restricted or unavailable due to YouTube's policies.
