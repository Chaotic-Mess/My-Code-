YouTube Scraper

Instantly grab metadata, thumbnails, transcripts, or shortcuts from any YouTube video — right from your browser.

How It Works

When you’re watching a YouTube video, click the bookmarklet below. A floating window will appear showing details such as:

  • Title, Channel, Views, Likes
  • Video description
  • Download or save options (MP4, MP3, transcript, shortcut)

The overlay runs fully in your browser — it doesn’t upload or send any data anywhere.

Install

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:

YouTube Scraper

When on a YouTube video page, click the “YouTube Scraper” bookmark to open the overlay.

Features

  • Fetches video title, views, likes, upload date
  • Grabs thumbnail, description, and transcript
  • Offers multiple download or save formats
  • Graceful fallbacks if a download option isn’t available (shortcut file)
  • Matches the Helpful Utilities dark theme

About
