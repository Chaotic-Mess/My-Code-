YouTube Scraper

Quickly capture metadata, download links, or transcripts from any YouTube video — directly in your browser.

How It Works

While watching a YouTube video, click the bookmarklet below. It will show a floating panel containing:

  • Video title, channel, views, likes
  • Available download formats (video/audio/transcript)
  • Copy-to-clipboard buttons for each field
  • Fallbacks for thumbnails, JSON metadata, or shortcuts

Install

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:

🎥 YouTube Scraper

Click this bookmark on any youtube.com/watch?v=... page to open the overlay.

Features

  • Detects available video/audio streams (resolutions, codecs)
  • Copy metadata or URLs to clipboard
  • Download thumbnails, JSON, transcripts, or shortcut files
  • All operations happen locally in your browser
